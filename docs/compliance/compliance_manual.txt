1. DATA PRIVACY AND GDPR COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------
ACME Corporation is committed to full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR) and applicable data protection laws. The following policies govern data handling:

Data Retention Schedules:
  - Employee personal data:        3 years after end of employment
  - Customer transaction records:  7 years (financial regulatory requirement)
  - System audit logs:             2 years
  - Marketing consent records:     3 years after consent withdrawal
  - Support ticket data:           2 years after ticket closure
  - Recruitment data (unsuccessful): 6 months after decision

Data Subject Rights:
  Individuals have the right to: access their data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion
  ("right to be forgotten"), restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing.
  All requests must be fulfilled within 30 calendar days. Requests should be submitted to
  privacy@acme.com. Identity verification is required before processing any access request.

Data Processing Basis:
  ACME processes personal data under the following lawful bases:
  - Contract performance (employee and customer contracts)
  - Legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security monitoring)
  - Consent (marketing communications)
  - Legal obligation (financial and tax records)

Cross-Border Data Transfers:
  Data may only be transferred outside the EEA using approved mechanisms: Standard Contractual
  Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs). All cross-border
  transfers must be approved by the Data Protection Officer (DPO) at dpo@acme.com.

2. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
---------------------------------
ACME maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and ISO 27001 accreditation.

Access Control:
  - All systems access follows the principle of least privilege
  - Privileged access (admin/root) requires Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  - Access is reviewed and recertified quarterly
  - Terminated employee access is revoked within 2 hours of HR notification
  - Shared/generic accounts are prohibited; all access must be individual and auditable

Password Policy:
  - Minimum 12 characters
  - Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
  - Passwords expire every 90 days
  - Last 12 passwords may not be reused
  - Password manager (1Password) is provided to all employees

Encryption:
  - All data in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher
  - All data at rest encrypted using AES-256
  - Encryption keys managed via AWS KMS; rotated annually
  - Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for Mac)

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
-----------------------------
ACME follows a structured incident response process based on NIST SP 800-61.

Incident Classification:
  P1 (Critical): Active breach, ransomware, or data exfiltration in progress
  P2 (High):     Confirmed unauthorized access, significant data exposure risk
  P3 (Medium):   Suspicious activity, potential vulnerability exploitation
  P4 (Low):      Policy violations, minor security misconfigurations

Response SLAs:
  P1: Incident response team engaged within 15 minutes; containment within 1 hour
  P2: Acknowledgment within 1 hour; containment within 4 hours
  P3: Acknowledgment within 4 hours; remediation within 72 hours
  P4: Acknowledgment within 1 business day; remediation within 2 weeks

Regulatory Notification Requirements:
  - GDPR: Supervisory authority must be notified within 72 hours of a breach involving
    personal data (Article 33). Affected individuals notified without undue delay if risk is high.
  - Financial data breach: SEC/FinCEN notification within 30 days (if applicable)
  - State breach notification: varies by state (14–90 days depending on jurisdiction)

Report an incident to: security@acme.com or call the 24/7 Security Hotline: 1-800-555-CERT

4. THIRD-PARTY VENDOR MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------------
All third-party vendors with access to ACME systems or data must complete the vendor
approval process before engagement.

Vendor Approval Process:
  Step 1: Business owner submits a Vendor Request Form in ServiceNow
  Step 2: Procurement reviews commercial terms and pricing (SLA, insurance, liability)
  Step 3: Security team conducts a security assessment via a standardized questionnaire
            (based on SIG Lite or CAIQ depending on criticality)
  Step 4: Legal reviews data processing agreements and contract terms
  Step 5: If vendor handles personal data, DPO reviews and approves DPA (Data Processing Agreement)
  Step 6: CISO signs off on vendors classified as "Critical" or "High" risk
  Step 7: Vendor is onboarded and added to the Approved Vendor Registry

Ongoing Monitoring:
  - All vendors are reviewed annually
  - Critical vendors (Tier 1) undergo on-site or third-party audit every 2 years
  - Vendors must notify ACME within 24 hours of any security incident that may affect ACME data
  - Vendors failing re-assessment must remediate within 90 days or face contract termination

5. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE MONITORING
-------------------------------------
Internal Audits:
  - Conducted quarterly by the Internal Audit team
  - Scope: access reviews, policy compliance, financial controls, IT general controls
  - Findings rated: Critical, High, Medium, Low
  - Critical findings must be remediated within 30 days

External Audits:
  - Annual SOC 2 Type II audit by an accredited CPA firm (currently Deloitte)
  - Annual ISO 27001 surveillance audit
  - Penetration testing conducted twice per year by an approved third party
  - Results of external audits are presented to the Board of Directors

Audit Readiness:
  - All employees must cooperate with audit requests
  - Evidence must be retained for a minimum of 3 years
  - Audit logs must not be modified or deleted; violations are treated as P2 incidents

6. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
--------------------------
ACME corporate systems (laptops, email, cloud services) are provided for business use.
Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not:
  - Violate any law or regulation
  - Consume excessive bandwidth or storage
  - Expose ACME systems to security risks
  - Involve streaming, gaming, or cryptocurrency mining

Prohibited uses include: accessing or sharing illegal content, installing unlicensed software,
circumventing security controls (VPN bypass, disabling endpoint protection), storing sensitive
data on personal devices, and using personal email for business communications.

ACME reserves the right to monitor and audit the use of corporate systems in accordance with
applicable law and with reasonable notice to employees.

7. BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY
----------------------------------------------
Recovery Objectives:
  - RTO (Recovery Time Objective): 4 hours for Tier 1 systems, 24 hours for Tier 2
  - RPO (Recovery Point Objective): 1 hour for Tier 1 systems, 24 hours for Tier 2

Backups:
  - Database backups run hourly (incremental) and daily (full)
  - Backups are stored in a geographically separate AWS region (us-west-2)
  - Backup restoration is tested quarterly

BCP Testing:
  - Tabletop exercises: twice per year
  - Full failover drill: annually
